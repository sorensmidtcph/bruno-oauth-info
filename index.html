Bruno

Bruno is Søren Smidt's private automation for Google Workspace. It helps with calendar summaries, email triage, accounting receipts, calendar availability and meeting notes.

Google data

The integration accesses only the Google accounts that Søren connects. Depending on the account, it may read Gmail and Calendar data, create or update calendar events, send user-approved emails, and create documents in Google Drive.

Availability

This is a private tool for Søren Smidt. It is not offered as a public service.
